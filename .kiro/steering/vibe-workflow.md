# Vibe Workflow

A lightweight, flow-oriented approach that prioritizes momentum over ceremony. Minimal upfront planning, brief post-task summary.

## During Task Execution

- Move fast and iterate
- Skip the detailed planning phase
- Focus on getting things done
- Ask clarifying questions inline if needed

---

## After Every Task (Summary Phase)

1. **What was implemented** — One or two sentences describing what you built
2. **Files changed** — Quick list of modified files
3. **How to test it** — One-liner or quick command to verify

---

## Workflow Override

If the user references `#planning-workflow` in their prompt,
switch to the Planning Workflow — do full upfront planning,
wait for approval before writing any code, and provide a 
detailed post-task summary.
