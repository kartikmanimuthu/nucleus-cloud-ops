8. Return `NextResponse.json({ success: true, data: updated })`.
9. Wrap in try/catch, return 500 on error with `{ success: false, error: message }`.
10. Add console.log at entry: `'API - PATCH /api/settings/members/[memberId] - Updating member role'`.
